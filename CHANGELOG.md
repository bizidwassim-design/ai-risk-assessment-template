## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-31

### Added
- Initial public release.
- `AI-Risk-Assessment-Template.xlsx` with six sheets (methodology, AI system inventory, risk scales, AI risk
  register, impact-assessment summary, dashboard), generated by `scripts/build_template.py`.
- Eight fictional AI risks across three fictional AI systems.
- `docs/iso42001-mapping.md`: how the workbook supports ISO/IEC 42001 clauses 6.1.2 and 6.1.4 and Annex A.5 / A.6 / A.7.
- `docs/eu-ai-act-quick-classifier.md`: plain-language decision tree for a first EU AI Act classification.
- `docs/ai-risk-categories.md`: the eight risk categories with prompts and example scenarios.
- Continuous integration: rebuilds the workbook, checks sheet names, macros and formula shape.
